Real recordings. Open licenses.

The app uses the local recordings below when installed. Until then, each engine uses a distinct synthesized preview. These are inspired sound profiles, not exact recreations of the named cars.

  • Classic Car Idle.wav — jackthemurray. 350 Rocket V8.
  • Porsche idle.aif — victormalloy.
  • Ferrari Engine (passive) — EwanPenman11.

All three recordings are released under CC0 1.0. Playback loops, pitch, and filtering are adapted in the browser. Exhaust pops and fallback sounds are synthesized.
