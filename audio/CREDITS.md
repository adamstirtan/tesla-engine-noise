# Audio sources

These sources were verified as CC0 1.0 on November 3, 2026. Run `python3 scripts/download_audio.py` from the repository root to install their public MP3 previews, or download the source files yourself, convert to MP3, and save them using the filenames below.

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The app creates seamless excerpts and adapts their playback rate and filtering. The sound profiles are stylized: they are not calibrated engine recordings across RPM/load, and the V8 recording is not a Camaro SS recording. Exhaust pops, the limiter effect, and the clearly labeled fallback previews are generated by the app.

Recordings are not bundled yet: the environment's automatic approval service failed before network downloads could execute. Missing recordings fall back independently to synthesized previews. If any sources change their licensing or become unavailable, the installer stops rather than guessing a download URL.
